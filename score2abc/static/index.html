    How it works
    
      
    
        
    1. Recognise
    Audiveris, an open-source optical music recognition engine, finds staves, notes, rests and symbols and writes MusicXML.
    
        
    2. Convert
    MusicXML is translated to ABC: each staff and voice becomes an ABC voice, with key, meter, ties, tuplets, repeats and dynamics.
    
        
    3. Check
    The score is drawn and played in your browser with abcjs, so you can spot and correct mistakes.
    
      
    
      
    For the best results
    
      
      
        
    • Use printed (engraved) music. Handwritten scores are not supported.
      • 
        
    • Scan at 300 DPI, or photograph the page flat, straight on and evenly lit.
      • 
        
    • Upload pages in order; they are joined into one piece.
      • 
        
    • A page takes about 20–60 seconds to recognise.
      • 
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    Uploads are deleted within an hour. Open source (MIT), built on Audiveris and abcjs.
